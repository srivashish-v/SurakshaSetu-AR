# Jury Demo Guide

## Objective

Show one complete training cycle rather than explaining every feature separately.

```text
Train → Act → Assess → Review → Retrain
```

## Demo Sequence

### 1. Open AR Training
Say:
> "We begin with the worker entering an assigned safety-training module."

### 2. Scan Marker
Say:
> "The prototype uses browser-based AR to place the training scenario into the user's environment."

### 3. Identify Falling-Rock Hazard
Say:
> "Instead of only watching a safety video, the worker has to observe the environment and identify the hazard."

### 4. Make Safety Decision
Say:
> "The trainee must make a decision based on the situation."

### 5. Complete Module 1
Say:
> "The assessment engine records the trainee's performance and produces a result."

### 6. Select Fire / Methane Module
Say:
> "The same framework can support multiple safety scenarios."

### 7. Complete Assessment
Show:
- Score
- Correct answers
- Incorrect answers
- Completion
- Performance information

### 8. Open Worker Application
Say:
> "The worker can track completed training and performance."

### 9. Open Mentor Dashboard
Say:
> "The mentor gets a separate view of worker performance."

### 10. Show Result
Show:
- Module completion
- Score
- Weak areas
- Performance information

Say:
> "The mentor receives structured information to review the worker's performance."

### 11. Show Feedback / Retraining

```text
Weak Area
   ↓
Mentor Review
   ↓
Retraining Assigned
   ↓
Worker Repeats Module
```

Say:
> "If the worker has a weak area, the mentor can assign targeted retraining."

### 12. Show Certification Flow
Say:
> "After the required training and competency validation, the worker can proceed through the certification workflow."

## Recommended 30-Second Explanation

> "Our prototype demonstrates how SurakshaSetu converts safety theory into practical decision-making. The worker enters an AR scenario, identifies a hazard, makes a decision and completes the task. The system records the assessment result, which can then be viewed by the mentor. If a weak area is identified, targeted retraining can be assigned. This creates a complete loop from learning to practice, assessment and improvement."

## Demo Backup Plan

If AR tracking fails:
1. Show the preloaded scenario.
2. Demonstrate the interaction.
3. Show the assessment result.
4. Open the mentor dashboard.
5. Explain the production AR transition.

The objective is to demonstrate the complete workflow rather than depend on one AR interaction working perfectly.
