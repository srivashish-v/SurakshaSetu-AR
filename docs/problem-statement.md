# SurakshaSetu Training Workflow

## Complete Training Loop

```text
LEARN
  ↓
EXPERIENCE
  ↓
ACT
  ↓
ASSESS
  ↓
FEEDBACK
  ↓
IMPROVE
  ↓
CERTIFY
```

### 1. LEARN
The worker receives the knowledge required for the module:
- Safety rules
- SOPs
- Hazard information
- Precautions
- Scenario context

### 2. EXPERIENCE
The worker enters an interactive AR scenario.

```text
Environment → Hazard Introduced → Worker Observes
```

### 3. ACT
The worker may:
- Identify a hazard
- Select an action
- Interact with an object
- Follow an evacuation route
- Answer a safety question
- Make a safety decision

### 4. ASSESS
The system records:

```text
Decision
Action
Mistake
Response Time
Question Answer
Task Completion
```

### 5. FEEDBACK
The system can highlight correct actions, incorrect actions, missed hazards, and areas requiring improvement.

### 6. IMPROVE
Mentors can review weak areas and assign targeted retraining.

### 7. CERTIFY
After required training and competency demonstration, the mentor can validate completion.

## Complete Operational Cycle

```text
Mentor Assigns Module
        ↓
Worker Logs In
        ↓
Worker Opens Assigned Training
        ↓
AR Scenario Starts
        ↓
Worker Observes Environment
        ↓
Worker Identifies Hazard
        ↓
Worker Makes Decision
        ↓
Worker Performs Action
        ↓
Assessment Engine Records Performance
        ↓
Performance Analysis
        ↓
Mentor Reviews Result
        ↓
Weak Area Identified
        ↓
Targeted Retraining
        ↓
Worker Repeats Scenario
        ↓
Competency Demonstrated
        ↓
Mentor Validation
        ↓
Certification
```
