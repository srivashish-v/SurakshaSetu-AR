# Problem Statement

## Overview

Mining and industrial safety training is often dependent on theoretical instruction, classroom sessions, demonstrations, and limited practical exposure.

Understanding a safety procedure theoretically does not always mean that a trainee can correctly identify a hazard, make a decision, and respond appropriately in a real-world situation.

SurakshaSetu addresses this gap through interactive, scenario-based AR training.

## What Is the Problem?

- Limited opportunities for repeated practical practice
- Difficulty recreating hazardous situations safely
- Dependence on physical training environments
- Manual evaluation of trainee performance
- Difficulty tracking individual weaknesses
- Limited access to realistic industrial scenarios
- Language and accessibility barriers
- Difficulty standardizing scenarios across batches

The resulting gap is:

**Learning Safety Procedures → Applying Them in Practice**

## Who Faces It?

### Workers / Trainees
They may receive theoretical instruction but have limited opportunities to repeatedly practice decision-making in realistic scenarios.

### Safety Trainers / Mentors
They need to observe performance, identify weaknesses, provide feedback, and conduct retraining.

### Mining & Industrial Organizations
They need scalable, standardized training and records of worker competency and progress.

## Why Existing Training Has Limitations

Physical practical training can be expensive to organize, difficult to repeat, dependent on infrastructure, time-consuming for trainers, and difficult to standardize.

Video-based learning can explain procedures but generally does not require the trainee to actively make decisions inside a simulated situation.

## Why Practical Competency Matters

Safety training should not only measure whether information was learned.

It should also help answer:

> Can the worker recognize the situation, make an appropriate decision, and perform the required action?

SurakshaSetu focuses on:

**Knowledge → Practice → Decision → Assessment → Improvement**

## Our Approach

The platform enables trainees to:
1. Learn the safety concept
2. Experience a simulated scenario
3. Identify hazards
4. Make decisions
5. Perform actions
6. Receive performance feedback
7. Repeat weak areas
8. Demonstrate competency

The goal is not to replace real-world training, but to make practical safety learning more repeatable, observable, measurable, accessible, and scalable.
